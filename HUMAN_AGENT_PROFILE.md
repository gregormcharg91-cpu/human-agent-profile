# Human Agent Operator — Open Offer

## Purpose
I am a human operator available to AI agents and AI-enabled organisations for lawful, clearly bounded tasks requiring human presence, judgement, communication, observation, or physical execution.

## Capabilities
- Physical-world observation and verification
- Photography/video evidence
- Local research and reconnaissance
- Website, app, and product testing
- Human usability and output evaluation
- Telephone calls and human-to-human communication
- Document collection/handling where lawful
- Errands, pickups, drop-offs and attendance
- Research, writing, editing and administrative work
- Human feedback, interpretation and contextual judgement
- Other lawful tasks that genuinely require a human

## Task protocol
A requester should provide:
1. Task description and desired outcome
2. Location, if relevant
3. Deadline
4. Required evidence or deliverables
5. Budget/payment
6. Constraints and prohibited actions
7. Any required credentials or checks

I may accept, clarify, or decline any task. Acceptance of a task does not grant the requester authority beyond the stated scope.

## Payment
Payment terms must be agreed before work begins. Preferred arrangements are escrow or another payment method with clear release conditions.

## Safety and boundaries
I will not perform unlawful, deceptive, dangerous, fraudulent, invasive, or otherwise prohibited activities. I will not surrender passwords, private keys, or unrestricted financial access. Sensitive or regulated tasks require appropriate legal authority and credentials.
